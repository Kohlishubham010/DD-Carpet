Our Story

The Foundation of Our Journey

Carpet Planet began with a simple realization—choosing the right carpet shouldn’t feel complicated.

We saw people struggling with limited options, unclear pricing, and little guidance. Something that has the power to transform a space was often reduced to just another purchase. We knew it could be better. What started as a small step towards offering a wider, more thoughtful range soon turned into a larger mission—to help people find carpets that truly complement their homes, their style, and the way they live. Over time, Carpet Planet has grown into a place where customers don’t just buy carpets—they discover what feels right for their space.

Because to us, a carpet isn’t just décor. It’s what brings warmth, character, and life into a home.

THE PROBLEM WE SAW

Over time, one thing became clear—people often weren’t sure if they were making the right choice. With so many options available, it became difficult to judge quality, compare designs, or understand what would truly work in their space. What looked good in isolation didn’t always feel right once it reached home. This uncertainty made the entire experience more stressful than it needed to be. It wasn’t just about buying a carpet. It was about being confident in that decision. And that’s where the real gap existed.

Shows traditional handmade carpet production

the traditional handmade carpet manufacturing process, where an artisan carefully creates a carpet design by hand. The craftsman is using a tufting tool to insert colored threads into the backing fabric, forming a decorative pattern. The printed design sheet placed beneath the weaving area helps the artisan follow the exact pattern, ensuring accuracy and symmetry. Handmade carpets like this require high skill, patience, and attention to detail.

Our Process

  • Design pattern is printed on paper
  • Fabric backing is stretched on loom
  • Artisan inserts yarn using hand tool
  • Colors are added according to pattern
  • Carpet surface is formed gradually
